Mini-splits are ideal for homes without ductwork, additions, finished basements, or any room that's always too hot or too cold. Each indoor unit is independently controlled, so you only condition the spaces you're using.

  • Room-by-room zoning for personalized comfort
  • Quiet operation and lower energy bills
  • No ductwork or major remodeling required
