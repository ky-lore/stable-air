Efficiency Savings

What SEER means
for your bills.

SEER (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio) measures how efficiently your AC or heat pump cools over a season. The higher the SEER, the less energy it takes to keep you comfortable — and the more you save month after month.

Upgrading from an older, lower-SEER system to a modern high-efficiency unit can meaningfully cut your cooling costs. During your free estimate, we'll show you the projected savings for your home and help you choose the right efficiency level for your budget.

Estimate My Savings

Older systems (10–13 SEER)

Common in homes 12+ years old — typically the least efficient and most expensive to run.

Standard new (14–16 SEER2)

Meets current efficiency standards — a solid, budget-friendly upgrade with real savings.

High-efficiency (17+ SEER2)

Maximum comfort and lowest operating cost — often paired with the best rebates.
